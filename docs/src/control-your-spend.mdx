---
title: Control your spend
description: The settings, limits and emails that keep usage and credits predictable
---

Usage is paid from dollar-based credits (see [Pricing](/pricing)). These are the safeguards that keep spend predictable.

## Keep sessions short

Sessions close on their own after an idle timeout or a maximum duration. Both are set per session.

| Setting | Default | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle_timeout_minutes` | 3 | 30 |
| `max_duration_minutes` | 15 | 1440 |

Set them to the shortest values your workflow needs. See [Session lifecycle](/features/sessions/lifecycle#2-set-appropriate-timeouts) for details.

## Turn off proxies you don't need

Built-in proxies are on by default and are billed per GB. Set `proxies` to `false` for sessions that don't need them. See [Proxies](/features/sessions/proxies#billing) for rates.

## Plan allowances

Some plans cap spend per source: LLM, proxy, browser sessions, workflow runs and agent tool calls. The **Included usage** card on the Limits page under [Billing](https://console.notte.cc/billing) in the console shows how much of each allowance you have used and when it resets.

When an allowance is used up:

* **With a payment method on file**, further usage is billed to your card at the end of the period.
* **Without one**, new work for that source is rejected with a `402` until the period resets, you add a payment method, or you upgrade your plan.

## Emails

Notte sends an email when:

* 80% of your plan's credits are used.
* Your credits run out. New sessions are blocked and scheduled functions are paused until you top up.

Top up from the [billing page](https://console.notte.cc/billing).

## What doesn't exist yet

There is no workspace-level monthly spending cap that you set yourself. Limits come from your plan's allowances and your credit balance.
