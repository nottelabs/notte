## Overview

[Quaso](https://www.quaso.ai) works great on mobile as a Progressive Web App (PWA):
you install it directly from your browser and it behaves like a native app - its
own icon on your home screen, a full-screen window without browser chrome, and
you stay signed in. No app store, no downloads, and it is always up to date
because the app *is* the website.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quaso in Safari">
    Go to [quaso.ai](https://www.quaso.ai) and sign in. The install option is
    only available in Safari - it won't appear in Chrome or Firefox on iOS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Share button">
    Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) in the Safari
    toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap
    **Add** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch from your home screen">
    Quaso now has its own icon on your home screen. Opening it gives you a
    full-screen, app-like experience with your session intact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Android (Chrome)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quaso in Chrome">
    Go to [quaso.ai](https://www.quaso.ai) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the browser menu">
    Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Tap **Add to Home screen** (on some devices this shows as **Install
    app**), then confirm with **Install** or **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch from your home screen">
    Quaso appears on your home screen and in your app drawer, and opens in its
    own window like any other app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Other Android browsers work too: in Samsung Internet use **Menu → Add page
  to → Home screen**; in Firefox use **Menu → Install**.
</Note>

## On your Mac

Prefer a desktop app? Quaso also ships a native macOS app - grab it at
[quaso.ai/download](https://www.quaso.ai/download). On macOS Sonoma and later
you can alternatively install the PWA from Safari via **File → Add to Dock**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The icon shows a letter instead of the croissant** - the icon was cached
  when you first added it. Remove the app from your home screen and add it
  again.
- **I don't see "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone** - make sure you're in Safari
  itself, not an in-app browser (e.g. opening the link from Slack or
  Instagram). Open Safari and navigate to quaso.ai directly.
- **The app looks outdated** - PWAs update on launch. Close it fully and
  reopen; if it persists, remove and reinstall the app (your account and data
  live on the server and are never affected).
